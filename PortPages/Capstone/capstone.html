2026 Capstone Project: Grit Hydroponics

← Back to portfolio

Status: in progress (Capstone I). Expected completion April 2027. Last updated November 2026.

UVU's Grit Gardens grows fresh food for Fresh Food Fridays, but production stops every October when the outdoor season ends. My capstone team is building the control system for a mobile indoor hydroponic cart that monitors its own water chemistry and corrects it, so fresh food can keep coming through fall and winter.

The problem

UVU has no hydroponics unit on campus, and students have little daily contact with plants inside enclosed buildings. Grit Gardens already owns an indoor cart, but it runs on a basic timer with no sensors. If the water chemistry drifts or the tank runs dry, nobody knows until the roots die and the harvest drops.

The cart needs to:

  • Track pH, nutrient strength (EC), temperatures, humidity, and water level
  • Correct high pH automatically and sound an alarm for out-of-range readings
  • Warn before the tank runs dry
  • Stay mobile and safe around water and 120 V power

Team and my role

This is a joint project between the College of Engineering and the College of Science. Our four-person Mechatronics capstone team designs and builds the control system. A Botany research team works out what setpoints the plants need and runs the pilot grow. I am the student lead for the capstone team.

The approach

An industrial PLC runs the cart in a continuous loop: sense the water, decide whether it is in range, act (run the pump, dose acid, shut down on low water), and show everything on a screen. Water drips through two felt-pocket walls in 30-minute pump cycles and drains back to the tank, so the sensors see the whole system.

Designing for safety

The pump runs on 120 V inside a water tank, so safety shaped the design from the start. The pump outlet has GFCI protection, all sensors run on 24 V DC, and a hardwired E-stop cuts every output. Acid doses are capped, and dosing stops if the pH sensor fails. The tank sits low on the base so the cart resists tipping.
